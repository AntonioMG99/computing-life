# Computing Life — Mailing-list privacy notice Last updated: 9 October 2026 ## Who is responsible for your information? Computing Life is an independent scientific community connecting computational, theoretical and experimental researchers interested in living systems. Antonio Matas Gil is the data controller for this mailing list, responsible for deciding how subscriber information is collected and used. For questions about your information or to exercise your rights, contact **antoniomatasgil@gmail.com**. ## What information do we collect? When you subscribe, we collect: - Your email address. - Your name, if you choose to provide it. - Your subscription choice and the date of your signup. We may also retain correspondence relating to your subscription, such as requests to update your details or unsubscribe. ## Why do we use your information? We use your information to manage your subscription and send Computing Life announcements about seminars, workshops and other relevant community activities. Our lawful basis for this processing is your consent, given when you select the signup option and submit the form. Subscribing is voluntary. Your email address is necessary to receive announcements; providing your name is optional. We do not use your information for unrelated advertising, profiling or automated decision-making. ## Where is your information stored? Signup responses are collected through Microsoft Forms, currently hosted through Antonio Matas Gil’s UCL account. Responses remain stored in Microsoft Forms unless deleted. A copy of the mailing list is also stored locally on a computer managed by Antonio Matas Gil. Access to the locally stored copy is restricted to Antonio Matas Gil. The Computing Life website and this privacy notice are hosted on GitHub Pages. Subscriber information is not published on the website or uploaded to the public GitHub repository. ## Who has access to your information? Antonio Matas Gil manages the subscriber list. Microsoft and the providers used to send announcements process information as necessary to provide their services. The institutional account hosting the form may also be subject to authorised administrative access. We do not sell subscriber information or share it with other organisations for their own marketing. If additional organisers are given access to the list, or the list moves to another service, we will update this notice and inform subscribers before the change takes effect. ## How long do we keep your information? We retain your signup information while you remain subscribed and the mailing list remains active. If you unsubscribe, we stop sending announcements and delete your signup information from the active mailing list, locally stored subscription records and Microsoft Forms within 30 days. If the mailing list closes, we delete those records within 30 days. Any information that must be retained to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute will be limited to what is necessary and retained only for as long as required. ## How can you unsubscribe? You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing **antoniomatasgil@gmail.com** or replying to a Computing Life announcement asking to unsubscribe. No explanation is required. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. ## Your data protection rights Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you can request: - Access to your personal information. - Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information. - Deletion of your information. - Restriction of its processing. - A portable copy of information you provided. You also have the right to withdraw consent at any time. Please send requests to **antoniomatasgil@gmail.com**. We normally respond within one month. If a permitted extension is necessary, we will explain why. ## Complaints If you have concerns, please contact **antoniomatasgil@gmail.com**. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ ## Changes to this notice We will update this notice when our arrangements change. We will inform subscribers of material changes affecting the use of their information and seek fresh consent where required.
